Roshni: diabetic eye screening that runs on your device

For a primary-care diabetes clinic in Pakistan with no eye specialist. Choose one retinal photo; Roshni suggests one next step for the doctor to confirm: refer, retake the photo, or rescreen in 12 months. A poor photo never gets an all-clear, and never blocks a referral.

Everything runs inside this browser. The photo is never uploaded, and after the first visit it works without internet.

Prototype for a student hackathon. Not medical advice and not a diagnosis.
